Project 01 · DMAIC

Analyzing Material Readiness as a Constraint on Industrial Project Execution

A Green Belt project focused on the relationship between material availability and industrial project execution.

Problem

Industrial projects can lose productive time when required materials are unavailable, arrive late, require expediting, or are not identified as critical early enough. The project examines material readiness as a potential constraint on execution.

Objective

Develop a data-driven view of material readiness and identify where earlier visibility, clearer thresholds, or exception-based escalation could reduce avoidable waiting and schedule risk.

Measure

Potential measures include material readiness percentage, late material lines, days waiting for material, supplier lead-time variance, expedite frequency, and availability at defined project milestones.

Analyze
  • Pareto analysis of material-related delays
  • Lead-time distributions and variance
  • Material-category and supplier analysis
  • Delay frequency and concentration
  • Relationship between readiness and project timing
Improve
  • Earlier identification of critical materials
  • Defined material-readiness thresholds
  • Exception-based escalation
  • Standardized project-readiness reporting
Control

A recurring material-readiness dashboard with defined thresholds, ownership, and escalation rules would provide a practical control mechanism.

Data integrity: The portfolio does not claim historical savings or causal relationships until they are supported by project data. Where data are not yet available, the page describes the proposed analysis.
