Project 03 · Lean / 5S / Flow

Reducing Warehouse Movement Waste Through Layout Improvement

A real operational intervention focused on transportation, motion, space utilization, and material organization.

Situation

While managing a branch warehouse, I identified a large wooden structure that obstructed useful floor space and contained welding equipment and miscellaneous product that did not belong there.

Intervention

The structure was removed and its contents were relocated. Removal required three pieces of heavy machinery and four people working approximately eight hours each.

10 × 4 × 6 ftApproximate structure dimensions
≈40 sq ftApproximate floor footprint recovered
≈240 cu ftApproximate occupied volume removed
≈32 labor-hoursOne-time removal effort
Lean Lens
  • Transportation: remove an obstruction that can increase movement around the warehouse.
  • Motion: improve access and reduce unnecessary movement around stored material.
  • Space: recover approximately 40 square feet of floor footprint.
  • 5S: relocate material to more appropriate, controlled storage locations.
How I would measure it today
  • Travel distance per material movement
  • Material-handling trips
  • Pick/retrieval time
  • Handling touches
  • Warehouse space utilization
  • Frequency of movement through the affected area
Important distinction: The approximately 32 labor-hours describe the one-time removal effort; they are not presented as productivity savings. No unsupported percentage improvement is claimed.
