Project 02 · Lean Six Sigma

Reducing RFQ-to-Quote Production Time

A transactional process-improvement study focused on cycle time, information quality, handoffs, rework, and quote complexity.

RFQ received
Information review
Technical / commercial input
Pricing
Review
Quote issued
Problem

RFQ-to-quote cycle time can vary because of incomplete information, multiple handoffs, workload, quote complexity, and rework.

Objective

Understand where elapsed time accumulates and identify practical ways to reduce queue time and rework without reducing quote quality.

Measure
  • Total RFQ-to-quote cycle time
  • Touch time versus queue time
  • Number of handoffs
  • Rework occurrences
  • Missing-information delays
  • Quote complexity
Analyze
  • Process map and value-stream analysis
  • Cycle-time distribution
  • Pareto of delay causes
  • Bottleneck and handoff analysis
  • Complexity-based segmentation
Improve
  • Standardized RFQ intake
  • Information-completeness checklist
  • Quote triage by complexity
  • Standard templates
  • Complexity-based routing
  • Reduce unnecessary handoffs
Control

Track quote cycle time and key delay categories through a recurring process-performance review.

Data integrity: Historical cycle-time numbers are intentionally omitted until the underlying records are analyzed. The process structure above is the working analytical model.

Method
Lean Six Sigma

Domain
Transactional Process Improvement

Primary KPI
RFQ-to-quote cycle time

Secondary KPIs
Queue time, touch time, rework, handoffs
