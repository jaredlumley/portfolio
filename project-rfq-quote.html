Reducing RFQ-to-Quote Production Cycle Time

A transactional process-improvement secondment focused on eliminating queue time, input defects, and handoff friction in an internal contract manufacturing environment.

Context & Engagement

Seconded as a Lean Six Sigma Yellow Belt practitioner to an internal contract manufacturing unit to address severe quoting responsiveness bottlenecks. Extended lead times were creating commercial friction, delaying client decision-making, and consuming excessive engineering capacity on preventable rework.

Define

Goal: Overhaul the end-to-end RFQ-to-Quote transactional workflow to achieve consistent, sub-1-week quoting turnaround while reducing internal engineering rework and clarification loops.

  • Primary KPI: RFQ-to-Quote overall elapsed cycle time (turnaround lead time).
  • Baseline Problem: Quotation lead times averaged 4 weeks (28 calendar days) with high variability, resulting in lost commercial bids and heavy operational backlogs.
Measure
  • Baseline Turnaround: 4 weeks (28 days) historical average turnaround time.
  • Touch vs. Queue Ratio: Active touch time averaged only 3 to 5 hours per quote, revealing that >95% of total elapsed time was non-value-added queue time.
  • Input Quality (Defect Rate): ~65% of incoming RFQs lacked complete technical drawings, bill of materials (BOM) specs, or commercial terms at intake, triggering repeated Requests for Information (RFIs).
  • Handoff Density: Mapped up to 6 distinct departmental handoffs per RFQ cycle.
Analyze
  • Transactional Value Stream Mapping (VSM): Pinpointed primary bottlenecks at the preliminary engineering review and custom cost estimation steps.
  • Pareto Analysis of Delays: Identified that 72% of idle queue time was driven by incomplete customer inputs at intake and unsegmented quote routing.
  • Queue Misalignment: Standard/off-the-shelf contract manufacturing requests followed the exact same multi-stage engineering path as complex, highly engineered custom assemblies.
Improve
  • Standardized Intake Gate & Checklist: Deployed a mandatory RFQ completeness verification checklist at initial receipt. Non-conforming RFQs were flagged before entering the estimation queue.
  • Complexity-Based Triage Matrix: Established a 2-tier routing path: Fast-track Tier 1 (Standard Catalog / Low Complexity) vs. Tier 2 (Custom Contract Engineering), bypassing deep technical queues for routine requests.
  • Handoff Consolidation: Rationalized approval levels, reducing departmental handoffs from 6 to 2 core operational touchpoints.
  • Standard Estimating Templates: Created parameterized pricing templates to eliminate redundant calculation work for recurring manufacturing operations.
Control & Quantified Results
  • Lead Time Reduction: Compressed overall RFQ-to-Quote cycle time from 4 weeks average down to 1 week maximum (a 75%+ peak reduction in lead time).
  • First-Time Right (FTR) Intake: Improved complete intake documentation from ~35% to >90% on day 1.
  • Quoting Capacity: Quadrupled throughput capacity for routine contract quotes without increasing staff headcount.
  • Process Governance: Implemented ongoing weekly control metric tracking (Xbar-R / I-MR charts) integrated into regular operational reviews.
Impact Summary: By addressing root-cause intake defects and eliminating transactional queue waste during this contract manufacturing secondment, the workflow was transformed into a disciplined, high-velocity process delivering predictable, sub-1-week quotes.
